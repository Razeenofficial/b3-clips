About B3 Clips

by Beyond Three

A local tool that turns long videos into vertical clips with captions. Nothing is uploaded: you review and post every clip yourself.

Credits. Built with yt-dlp, ffmpeg, faster-whisper, OpenCV and the YuNet face model (opencv_zoo, MIT). Moment-picking rules adapted from AI-Youtube-Shorts-Generator (MIT) and OpenShorts (MIT). Reframing, caption and selection ideas inspired by OpenShorts, SupoClip (supoclip.com) and Clips Studio; no code was copied from the last two. Full notices: THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
